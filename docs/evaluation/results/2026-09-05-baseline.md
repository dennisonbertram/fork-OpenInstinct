## Findings supported by transcript and tool evidence

- **Order status was confused with pickup readiness.** CORE-10 initially answered
  about a different account order in all three trials. After the user named ORD_0,
  two trials explicitly said it was ready because it was completed. The fixture
  establishes completion, not pickup readiness; the third response is ambiguous.
- **Customer ambiguity was missed in all three SQ-05 trials.** Jory selected the
  first Ada without following the customer pagination cursor. Another Ada was on
  the next page. Later scripted clarification produced the right items and total,
  but did not erase the premature initial selection.
- **Revoked access recovery lacked the promised connection step in all three
  trials.** Jory referred to a sign-in prompt, but no authorization event or URL
  existed. Protected reads remained blocked. In contrast, the corrected
  never-connected fixture emitted actual authorization requests in all three
  trials; these were not completed authorizations. All stopped after dismissal.
- **Failure recovery was uneven.** All three SQ-08 trials eventually returned the
  correct $55.75 after explicit retry. Two exceeded the authored initial request
  limit; one made another failing lookup on the intervening status-only question.
  Another misstated five fetched pages as four. One recovery used payments rather
  than the order route required by the authored check.
- **A follow-up lost its referent.** In SQ-03 trial 2, the answer gave 12 overall
  units rather than the leading item's five units. The arithmetic was valid for
  a different question.

The sampled agent also narrowed plans on request, retained corrections, answered
basic arithmetic correctly, shortened drafts, respected several conversation
endings, and returned grounded sales, inventory, and invoice answers. These are
observations in the recorded synthetic cases, not population reliability claims.
Style choices such as joking after “serious now” belong in owner review.

## Why the automatic scores are advisory

Only 48 of the 63 usable conversations have valid-format judgments; 15 judge
errors remain ungraded. Review found judges mistaking drafts sent into the current
chat for external messages, missing facts present in the transcript, and reversing
the order of retry and dismissal. Old simplified captures omitted a clarification
question and authorization requests. Deterministic checks also rejected allowed
acknowledgement reactions and read-only email skill guidance.

The harness now captures those events and has regression coverage. It also supplies
clearer draft, chronology, and currency context for future judges. These changes
do not retroactively replace the original judgments. Preference-memory expectations
remain a product/evaluation policy question. No overall delight score or calibrated
quality threshold is claimed.

## Fixture and product limits

The Square fake ignores `closed_at` filtering, does not provide meaningful payment
pagination, lacks later-refund distractors and cross-location inventory counts,
and rejects some unsupported read endpoints with misleading 403 responses. Correct
totals therefore do not prove those real Square semantics. Selected-order client
context is available only to the next model call, which limits conclusions about
context surviving tool steps. Synthetic authorization tests establish local
framework requests, not OAuth login, seller grants, or rendered connection controls.

## Verification and next work

At integrated harness commit `d229e9f`, `pnpm check` passed with 1,241 tests passed
and two skipped; `pnpm build` passed; all 71 focused conversation tests passed;
`git diff --check` passed. The final paid Square regression gate ran 13 cases and
returned **Results: 5 passed, 8 failed (13 total)** with exit 1. Eight cases
encountered budget-guard rejections before their required model calls were
dispatched; smaller requests still ran, but the remaining authorization could not
cover conservative request reservations. The gate therefore does not fully
exercise those cases and is not green; the branch is not merge-ready.
These local results do not establish deployment or channel delivery.

The next behavior goal should address the supported readiness, customer discovery,
revoked-access recovery, retry, and follow-up failures using these preserved cases.
Owner ratings should precede any claim that automated quality judgments reflect
the desired warmth, smoothness, or delight. Changes to Jory's behavior were outside
this measurement goal.
